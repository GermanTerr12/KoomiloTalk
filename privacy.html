Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your child's words, routines, photos and settings stay on your device.
  • We never sell or share your family's information.
  • Backups are files you save yourself. We never see them.
  • If the app crashes, we get a technical report to fix the bug, with personal info turned off.
  • Questions? Email koomilotalk@gmail.com.

Who we are

Koomilo Talk is a communication and calm app for autistic kids and their families. It is made to be used by a child together with a parent, caregiver, therapist or teacher. In this policy, "we" and "us" means the Koomilo Talk team, and "you" means the caregiver who sets up the app.

We built Koomilo Talk to feel safe and predictable, and we want our privacy to feel the same way. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

What stays on your device

Everything you and your child create in Koomilo Talk stays on your phone or tablet. That includes:

  • Words and buttons on the talk board, including any you change
  • Routines and steps in My Day
  • Photos you add to buttons or routine steps
  • Settings, like voice, volume and sound choices

This information is kept in encrypted storage inside the app. We don't have a server that receives it, and we can't see it.

Photos

If you choose to add a photo, Koomilo Talk will ask permission to use your camera or photo library. Photos you pick are only used inside the app, on your device. They are never uploaded, shared or used for anything else. You can say no and still use every part of the app with the built-in pictures.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in. We never ask for your name, your child's name or a password.
  • No ads of any kind.
  • No tracking, analytics or advertising IDs.
  • No selling, renting or trading of anyone's information. Ever.

Backups

Caregivers can save a backup file and restore it later, for example when moving to a new device. You choose where the file goes, and you restore it yourself. We never receive your backup files.

A backup can hold your child's words, routines and photos. Please keep backup files somewhere safe. If you save one to a service like iCloud Drive or Google Drive, that service's own privacy policy applies to the file.

Crash reports

If Koomilo Talk crashes, the app sends a crash report to Sentry, a service that helps developers find and fix bugs. We have turned off Sentry's personal information collection.

A crash report includes technical details, like the device model, system version, app version and what part of the app had a problem. It does not include your child's words, routines, photos or settings. We use crash reports only to fix bugs and make the app more reliable.

Our website and waitlist

If you join the waitlist on our website, we collect your email address and, if you choose, your role (for example, parent or therapist). The form is handled by Formspree, which passes your answers on to us.

We use this only to send you Koomilo Talk launch news. We don't send ads and we don't share your email. You can ask to be removed at any time by emailing koomilotalk@gmail.com, and we'll delete your details.

Our website doesn't use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical details, like IP addresses, to keep the site secure and running. Links to Instagram and TikTok take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Koomilo Talk is made for children, so we take the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) seriously.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • Everything a child creates or uses in the app stays on the device.
  • The app has no accounts, chat, social features, ads or tracking.
  • Crash reports contain only technical details and are used only to keep the app working.
  • The website waitlist is meant for adults only.

If you're a parent or guardian and you think a child has sent us personal information, please email koomilotalk@gmail.com. We'll delete it promptly.

Your choices

  • Delete app data: deleting the app removes everything stored inside it from that device. Backup files you saved are yours to keep or delete.
  • Photos: you can turn camera and photo access on or off at any time in your device settings.
  • Waitlist: email us to be removed, and we'll delete your details.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Koomilo Talk handles information, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date at the top. If a change is important, we'll make it easy to notice before it takes effect.
