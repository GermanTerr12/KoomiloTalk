Everything in one calm place

Simple tools, soft colors and no surprises. Koomilo keeps things predictable and kind.

Talk board

Big, friendly picture buttons that speak out loud. Caregivers can customize the board to fit each child.

My Day routines

Visual schedules that show what is happening now and what comes next, so the day feels clear and predictable.

Timer

A soft visual timer for waiting, transitions and turn-taking. No loud alarms.

Calm corner

A quiet space with slow, gentle activities to help your child settle when things feel like a lot.

Sign & say with Koomilo

Koomilo shows simple signs and says the word out loud, so kids can learn, copy and practice together.

Who it's for

Koomilo Talk is made to be shared by the people who support each child.

Parents

A simple way to help your child be heard at home, on the go and at bedtime, set up the way that suits your family.

Therapists

A friendly tool to practice words, signs and routines between sessions, and to build on what you're already teaching.

Schools

Clear routines and calm tools that make classroom transitions smoother for students who need extra support.
